---
title: "`latest` image tags in production"
ring: hold
quadrant: techniques
what: Deploying containers referenced by mutable `:latest` tags instead of pinned versions or digests.
why: Breaks reproducibility, complicates rollbacks, and causes silent drift across cluster nodes.
action: Hold in all Compose and Kubernetes manifests; pin explicit semver tags or SHA256 digests.
