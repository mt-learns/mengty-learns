## TL;DR
Emit **structured JSON to `stdout`**, ship with a lightweight collector (Filebeat or Fluent Bit), parse/redact in an ingest pipeline, and store in **Elasticsearch data streams** managed by **ILM** (`hot → warm → cold → delete`). Correlate every log with `trace_id` and `request_id`, and mask PII/tokens before they ever hit disk.

## Pipeline & KQL quick reference

```text
app (JSON stdout) ─► Filebeat / Fluent Bit ─► Ingest Pipeline (enrich + redact) ─► Elasticsearch Data Stream (ILM) ─► Kibana
```

Useful KQL queries in Kibana Discover:

```text
service.name: "payment-service" and http.response.status_code >= 500
trace_id: "4bf92f3577b34da6a3ce929d0e0e4736"
log.level: "error" and not error.type: "ValidationError"
http.response.status_code: 504 and event.duration > 5000000000
```

## ILM & retention tiers
- **Hot:** Active writes on fast SSD/NVMe; rollover at size/age threshold (e.g. `50GB` or `1d`) so shards stay 20–50 GB.
- **Warm / Cold:** Read-only, force-merged, moved to cheaper spinning/object-backed nodes for compliance retention.
- **Delete:** Automatically purge indices after your retention policy expires (separate shorter retention for debug/access logs vs audit logs).

## Gotchas
- **Dynamic mapping explosions:** Logging arbitrary user payloads or dynamic map keys at the top level creates thousands of index fields, bloating cluster state across every node.
- **Tiny shard sprawl:** Creating daily indices for low-volume services leaves thousands of 5 MB shards; use **data streams** with size-based rollover (`max_primary_shard_size: 30gb`).
- **Logging secrets or PII:** Once a bearer token or PAN enters Elasticsearch, it is replicated across shards and snapshots; redact at the app logger *and* drop/mask in ingest pipelines.

## Production checklist
- [ ] Apps log single-line JSON to `stdout` with `timestamp`, `level`, `service`, `trace_id`, and `request_id`.
- [ ] Ingest pipeline or collector redacts `Authorization`, cookies, passwords, and PII patterns.
- [ ] Data streams use ILM policies with size-based rollover (avoiding per-day tiny shards).
- [ ] Security/audit logs sit in a separate append-only index/stream with longer retention than noisy debug logs.
