## TL;DR
Healthy engineering productivity means **sustainable delivery with low rework**, not maximum individual busyness. Pair **DORA throughput and stability trends** with **team-health signals** (WIP age, PR review time, on-call load, bus factor), and use them strictly to fix **system bottlenecks**—never to grade individuals.

## Why it exists
When teams only measure "tickets closed" or "lines of code," they reward oversized PRs, skipped tests, and burnout. Good metrics reveal where work actually stalls—such as 3-day PR review queues, flaky CI pipelines, or single-person knowledge silos.

## Mental model: Six balanced signal areas

| Area | Signals to watch | What it reveals |
|---|---|---|
| **Throughput (DORA)** | Deployment frequency, change lead time | How fast and smoothly small changes reach users |
| **Stability (DORA)** | Change failure rate, recovery time, rework rate | Whether speed is coming at the cost of outages or rollbacks |
| **Flow** | WIP age, PR review turnaround, blocked time | Where work queues up between coding and merge |
| **Quality** | Escaped defects, flaky tests, build failure rate | Strength of automated gates (`process/dor-dod-quality-gates`) |
| **Team health** | Unplanned interruptions, on-call pages, bus factor | Whether the current pace is sustainable |
| **Customer value** | Feature adoption, task completion, support volume | Whether shipped code solves real user problems |

## Do it: Run one improvement experiment at a time

```markdown
# Improvement Experiment: Cut PR Review Wait Time

- **Baseline:** Median time from PR open to first review is 26 hours.
- **Hypothesis:** Adding a 2-hour review SLA for PRs under 300 lines will cut cycle time without hurting quality.
- **Experiment:** For 2 sprints, split PRs > 400 lines and assign a review buddy at stand-up.
- **Result & Decision:** Median review wait dropped to 4 hours; adopt permanently.
```

### High-leverage fixes for common bottlenecks
- **Too much WIP:** Limit active tickets per person and unblock aging items before starting new ones.
- **Knowledge silos:** Ensure at least two engineers can deploy, debug, and support every critical service via pairing and rotations.
- **Meeting fragmentation:** Protect uninterrupted 2-hour maker blocks and move routine status async.

## Gotchas
- **Goodhart's Law:** The moment "PRs merged" or "deployment count" becomes an individual KPI, people game the number instead of helping teammates.
- **Optimizing throughput alone:** DORA research shows fast deployments without test automation and maintainability only accelerate rework.
- **Changing five things at once:** Fix one bottleneck per sprint so you can tell which change actually helped.

## Production checklist
- [ ] Metrics are reviewed as **team trends** monthly, never as individual scorecards.
- [ ] Throughput metrics are always paired with **stability** and **team-health** signals.
- [ ] Critical services have a bus factor ≥ 2 (two people who can deploy and debug).
- [ ] Every metric-driven change is run as a time-boxed experiment with a baseline.
