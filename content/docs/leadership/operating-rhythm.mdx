## TL;DR
A healthy **operating rhythm** gives a team predictable touchpoints for **alignment, quality, and growth** without drowning engineers in meetings. Keep routine status **async**, reserve live calendars for **decisions and collaboration**, and cancel any meeting that lacks an agenda.

## Team cadence cheatsheet

| Practice | Cadence | Timebox | Primary purpose | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Daily stand-up** | Daily | 10–15 min | Surface blockers and coordinate today's work | Team Lead |
| **Async status (RAG)** | Daily / Weekly | 5 min read | Share progress, risks, and decisions needed | Team Lead / PM |
| **Code review** | Continuous | Same-day SLA | Maintain quality, security, and shared context | Developers + Lead |
| **Backlog refinement** | Weekly | 45–60 min | Clarify scope, acceptance criteria, and dependencies | PO + Team Lead |
| **Design / ADR review** | As needed | 30 min | Align on architecture, schemas, and risks | Team Lead |
| **Sprint planning** | Per sprint | 60 min | Commit to realistic goals and clear owners | Lead + PO + PM |
| **Release readiness** | Pre-release | 15–30 min | Verify tests, migrations, rollback, and runbook | Lead + Ops + QA |
| **Sprint demo / review** | Per sprint | 30–45 min | Show working software and gather feedback | Lead + PO |
| **Retrospective** | Per sprint | 45 min | Turn friction into 1–2 owned process fixes | Team Lead |
| **1:1 check-in** | Biweekly / Monthly | 30 min | Coaching, feedback, workload, and growth | Team Lead |
| **Health & metric review** | Monthly | 30–45 min | Inspect DORA, defect trends, and team load | Lead + PM |

## Meeting hygiene rules
1. **No agenda, no meeting:** State the goal and decision needed in the invite before people accept.
2. **Minimum viable attendee list:** Invite only people needed for the decision; send notes to everyone else (`Informed`).
3. **Separate reading from deciding:** Share context docs 24 hours ahead so live time goes to trade-offs, not reading slides aloud.
4. **Start and end on time:** Respect makers' schedules (`25`/`50`-minute defaults leave breathing room).
5. **Close with owners:** End every meeting with written decisions, owners, and due dates.

## Minimum viable version (1–3 person team)
- **Monday (15 min):** Pick the week's 2–3 outcomes and flag blockers.
- **Daily (async):** 3-line stand-up message in chat (*Done / Next / Blocked*).
- **Friday (20 min):** Demo what shipped and write one retro action item for next week.

## Gotchas
- **Calendar creep:** Adding a new recurring sync after every minor hiccup eventually leaves engineers with zero 2-hour coding blocks. Audit recurring meetings quarterly and delete stale ones.
- **Stand-ups that become status reports to the lead:** Stand-up is for teammates to coordinate with each other, not to recite ticket IDs to a manager.

## Production checklist
- [ ] Every recurring team meeting has a clear owner, purpose, and timebox.
- [ ] Routine status updates happen asynchronously rather than in live calls.
- [ ] Engineers have protected, uninterrupted focus blocks on the calendar.
- [ ] Every retro and metric review produces at least one tracked, owned improvement.
