## TL;DR
During an active outage, an engineering leader's job is **coordination, calm, and fast mitigation—not hero debugging**. Keep the **Incident Commander** role separate from hands-on investigation, send **fact-only status updates** with a next-update timestamp, and ensure postmortem prevention actions are tracked to completion.

## Why it exists
In high-stress outages, teams often crowd into one screen-share while nobody updates stakeholders, nobody records decisions, and two engineers accidentally run conflicting fixes. Clear incident leadership restores service faster and protects trust afterward.

## Mental model: You build it, you own how it runs
Pair the operational playbook in [Incident Management](/docs/process/incident-management) with explicit leadership boundaries:
- **Stabilize before root-cause hunting:** Roll back, toggle a feature flag, or isolate the failing dependency first.
- **Command vs execution:** The IC directs and unblocks; investigators and ops run commands.
- **Facts only externally:** Never speculate about root cause in stakeholder updates until logs confirm it.

## Do it: Incident RACI and update template

### 1. Incident RACI for small engineering teams

| Activity | Team Lead / IC | Developer | Ops / SRE | PM / Comms | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Declare severity & assign roles** | **A / R** | C | C | I | C |
| **Stabilize / rollback service** | A | **R** | **R** | I | I |
| **Investigate logs & root cause** | A | **R** | **R** | I | C |
| **Communicate customer impact** | C | I | C | **A / R** | C |
| **Write & close postmortem actions** | **A** | **R** | **R** | I | C |

### 2. Fact-only incident update template

```text
Incident status: Investigating / Mitigating / Monitoring / Resolved

Impact:
- Checkout API returning 502 for ~30% of EU requests since 14:05 UTC.

Confirmed facts:
- Error rate spiked 3 minutes after release #418; database CPU is normal.

Actions in progress:
- Rolling back release #418 to #417 (Owner: @oncall-ops).

Owner:
- @team-lead (IC)

Next update:
- 14:25 UTC (in 15 minutes)
```

## Gotchas
- **The IC grabbing the keyboard:** The moment the lead starts debugging, communication stops and duplicate work begins.
- **Skipping rollback rehearsal:** A rollback plan written in a wiki that nobody has tested under pressure often fails during a real outage.
- **Unclosed postmortems:** A postmortem with five action items and zero tracked tickets guarantees the same outage repeats next quarter.

## Production checklist
- [ ] On-call engineers know who holds **IC**, **Mitigation**, and **Comms** roles before an outage hits.
- [ ] Rollback and feature-flag kill switches are rehearsed on staging.
- [ ] Critical side effects (payments, inventory, webhooks) use idempotency keys so retries are safe.
- [ ] Every SEV1/SEV2 postmortem action item is reviewed weekly until closed.
