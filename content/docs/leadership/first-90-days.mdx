## TL;DR
Don't try to overhaul the entire engineering org in week one. Use **Days 1–30** to learn the system and build clarity habits, **Days 31–60** to formalize RACI, ADRs, and delegation, and **Days 61–90** to ship a measurable reliability or delivery improvement.

## Days 1–30: Listen, learn, and establish clarity
- [ ] Map the current idea-to-production flow end to end (where do tickets wait?).
- [ ] Hold 1:1 listening sessions with every teammate, PM, PO, QA, and Ops partner.
- [ ] Publish or clarify lightweight **DoR / DoD** (`process/dor-dod-quality-gates`) and PR checklists.
- [ ] Write a 1-page **feature brief** (`leadership/clarity-and-alignment`) for one upcoming initiative.
- [ ] Start consistent **async RAG status updates** (`leadership/communication-and-listening`).
- [ ] Keep a short Friday reflection: *outcomes, risks, decisions, lessons, next week's top 3*.

## Days 31–60: Build ownership and quality flow
- [ ] Publish a role-based **RACI matrix** (`process/raci`) for feature delivery and releases.
- [ ] Lead one technical design review and document the choice in an **ADR** (`process/adr`).
- [ ] **Delegate one real outcome** end to end with clear constraints and check-in dates (`leadership/delegation-and-ownership`).
- [ ] Time-box a technical spike before committing to an uncertain estimate.
- [ ] Baseline key flow signals: PR review turnaround, WIP age, and escaped defects.
- [ ] Start recurring 1:1s with one realistic growth goal per teammate.

## Days 61–90: Lead sustainable improvements
- [ ] Steward one cross-functional feature from discovery through production verification.
- [ ] Run a **blameless retrospective** (`process/retrospectives`) and close at least one owned action item.
- [ ] Improve one operational guardrail (CI check, rollback script, runbook, or alert).
- [ ] Run a short incident tabletop rehearsal (`leadership/incident-leadership`).
- [ ] Audit recurring meetings and protect uninterrupted deep-work blocks.
- [ ] Gather concrete feedback from your manager, engineers, and PM/PO peers.

## Gotchas
- **Changing everything in week one:** Introducing five new templates before understanding why current habits exist triggers resistance. Fix one visible pain point first.
- **Staying in "observer mode" too long:** By Day 45, the team needs visible decisions, an ADR, and unblocked PRs—not just listening notes.

## Production checklist
- [ ] Your 90-day goals are tied to **real team deliverables**, not abstract reading lists.
- [ ] You have a recurring Friday 15-minute review with yourself and monthly check-in with your manager.
- [ ] Every new process habit (brief, RACI, retro action) has a clear owner and low overhead.
